## Greeting Style
- Known personal contacts: "Hi {name}," or "Hello {name},"
- Professional/unknown: "Dear {name}," or "Hello {name},"
- Adam's preferred: Brief first name or full name depending on familiarity.
- Family: "Hi {name}," — always warm.

## Length Rules
- Job-lead: 3–5 sentences, cover key points.
- Recruiter: 2–3 sentences, declining clearly.
- Unemployment: 2–3 sentences, confirmatory.
- Personal: 1–2 sentences unless specific action required.
- Maximum: 5 sentences for any single reply.

## Common Phrases
- "Thank you for reaching out" — opening for professional contacts.
- "I'm very interested" — enthusiasm without commitment.
- "Could you please share" — polite request for information.
- "I'd be happy to connect" — openness to future contact.
- "Feel free to reach me here" — alternative contact channel.
- "Best regards, Adam" — standard sign-off.
- "Hi {name}" — informal opening for known contacts.

## Sign-off Rules
- Professional: "Best regards,\nAdam"
- Personal: "Best,\nAdam" or "Talk soon,\nAdam"
- Never use full name unless email demands it.
- Never include phone number unless Adam added it.

## Formality Scale
- Family/close friends: Warm, casual.
- Known personal services (bank, insurer): Polite, efficient.
- Unknown professional: Formal, complete sentences.
- Recruiter: Polite, appreciative, declining.
