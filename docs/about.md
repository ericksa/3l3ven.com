# About Eleven Entertainment

## Our Story

Founded by Adam Erickson, Eleven Entertainment has been delivering exceptional DJ services and music entertainment across Michigan and beyond. With a passion for music and a commitment to excellence, we've built a reputation for creating unforgettable experiences at weddings, corporate events, festivals, and private parties.

## The DJ

Adam Erickson brings over a decade of professional DJ experience, spinning everything from classic hip-hop and R&B to contemporary EDM and house music. Known for reading the crowd and adapting the energy of any event, Adam ensures every moment is perfectly timed and curated.

- Professional sound systems
- Lighting packages
- Microphones and communication equipment
