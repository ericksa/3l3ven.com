Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use 3l3ven.com, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any other contact details you provide when booking events, signing up for newsletters, or contacting us.

We use the collected information to:

  • Process and confirm bookings and events.

  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including tax and accounting requirements.

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We may share your data with trusted third‑party service providers who assist us in operating our site, processing payments, and delivering services. These providers are restricted to using your information only as necessary to provide their services to us, and they are obligated to maintain confidentiality and security.

4. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed to fulfill the purposes outlined above, or as required by law. Typically, booking data is retained for **seven (7) years** for financial and tax purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for any specific use of your data.
  • Receive a copy of your personal data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at aerickson@vettsolutions.com.
